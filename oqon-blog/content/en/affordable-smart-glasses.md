## What do you actually need?

Start from what you'll do every day, not from the spec sheet:

- **You mostly want to capture and listen:** photos, video, music and calls with your hands free. You don't need to pay for extras you won't use.
- **You want a voice assistant:** check which languages it handles well and what it needs to work (account, app, internet).
- **You wear prescription glasses:** find out who fits the lenses and what they cost on top.

Be honest with yourself about habits. If you'll wear them daily, comfort, lens options and sweat resistance matter more than a long feature list. If you only expect to use them on trips, a simpler pair may be plenty.

## What to check before you pay

1. **Where your files go.** Cloud upload, or straight to your phone?
2. **A light that shows when the camera is on.** It protects the people around you, and you.
3. **Phone compatibility.** Android and iPhone.
4. **Prescription support.** Cost and where lenses are fitted.
5. **Sweat and rain resistance.** For everyday use outdoors.
6. **Returns, warranty and support.** Where they apply and who answers.
7. **Payment and shipping.** What's accepted and where they ship.

## Common mistakes when buying smart glasses

Overpaying rarely comes down to the list price. It usually comes from decisions made with too little information:

- **Buying the brand instead of the use.** If you'll take photos, play music and answer calls, you don't need to pay for features you'll never touch.
- **Forgetting the prescription.** If you wear prescription lenses, ask up front what fitting them costs and who does it. That is part of the real price.
- **Not reading what the assistant needs.** A voice assistant requires an account, an app and internet on your phone. Better to know before you buy than after.
- **Skipping the return policy.** A clear window and a full refund remove the risk of getting it wrong.
- **Ignoring where your files go.** Your photos and video are yours; confirm whether they upload to a cloud or move straight to your phone.
