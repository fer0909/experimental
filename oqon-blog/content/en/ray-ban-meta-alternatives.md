## Seven questions to ask before you pick an alternative

A spec sheet won't tell you whether a pair of glasses will work for you. These questions will:

1. **What will you use them for?** If it's mostly photos, short videos, music and calls, you don't need the most expensive pair on the market.
2. **Where do your photos and videos go?** Ask whether they're uploaded to a cloud or move straight to your phone.
3. **Does the assistant speak your language well?** Ask which language it's optimized for and what it needs to work.
4. **Is there a light that shows when the camera is on?** It's the simplest way for the people around you to know what's happening.
5. **Do they work with your prescription?** The real cost includes fitting the lenses. Ask where and how much.
6. **Where is the warranty honored?** You want support from a real person, in a place you can actually reach.
7. **Can you return them?** A clear window with a full refund takes the risk out of trying.
