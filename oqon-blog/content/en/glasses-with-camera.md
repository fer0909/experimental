> Glasses with a camera let you take photos and record video from your own eye level without pulling out your phone. Most also include open-ear speakers for music and calls. In the U.S. the best-known pair, Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2, starts at $379; lower-priced options exist. Before you buy, check where your photos go, whether a light turns on while recording, whether they take a prescription, and where the warranty is honored.

## What problem do camera glasses solve?

They solve one specific problem: the moments you want to keep happen while your hands are busy. Unlocking your phone and opening the camera pulls you out of the moment, and often you're too late.

- **Capture without letting go:** while cooking, holding your kid, riding a bike or working.
- **Record in first person:** the video shows exactly what you saw, from your point of view.
- **Listen and talk with nothing in your ears:** music and calls through open-ear speakers in the arms.
- **Ask out loud:** models with a voice assistant answer in your ear, no screen needed.

## Who are they for?

- **Content creators:** first-person recipes, travel and experiences, no tripod.
- **Parents:** the game, the birthday, the first steps, without watching it all through a screen.
- **Field workers:** sales reps, technicians and warehouse teams who document with photos without putting their tools down.
- **Cyclists and travelers:** music, calls and a record of the ride with your hands free, always within traffic rules.

If you rarely take photos or video, camera glasses won't change your day. If you do it often and your hands are usually full, they will.

## Six things to check before you buy

1. **Where your photos and videos go.** Ask whether they upload to a cloud or go straight to your phone.
2. **A recording light.** An activity light that can't be turned off protects the people in front of you, and protects you.
3. **Prescription support.** Confirm who fits prescription lenses and what they cost on top.
4. **Sweat and rain resistance.** For everyday street use, look for at least splash protection.
5. **Warranty and support.** Where the warranty is honored and whether real people answer in your language.
6. **Payment.** Which methods are accepted and when you pay.
